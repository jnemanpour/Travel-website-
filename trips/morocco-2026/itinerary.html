Every day, every stop, every booking link in one place

How This Works

11 nights on the ground · a clean one-way arc with no backtracking

Each day below shows the date, where we are, what we're doing, where we're sleeping, and the exact links to book that day's pieces. Hotels are listed across their whole stay (e.g. "La Mamounia · 3 nights · Aug 24–27") so we only book each one once. Every night is booked and we have a driver for the whole trip — he meets us at the airport on Aug 24 and takes us back on Sep 4, so there are no transfers to arrange. The camel trek at Merzouga is the last piece of the core trip.

Shabbat note: Shabbat (Sat Aug 29) falls on the final day of our desert tour as we transfer to Fes — no synagogue visits are scheduled then. We've set our Fes Jewish-heritage day for Mon Aug 31, when the synagogues are open, and Casablanca's Jewish museum for the daytime of Thu Sep 3 (it's closed Saturdays).

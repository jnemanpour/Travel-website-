The Sahara Days

Days 4–6 · Marrakech → Fes via Merzouga · the heart of the trip

Nothing to book here any more ✓ — we didn't buy a packaged desert tour. Both nights are reserved directly (Eden Boutique in Boumalne Dades and Riad Madu at Merzouga) and our driver covers the road. The one desert experience still to arrange is below.
Sunset Camel Trek at Erg Chebbi Still to book~$25–60/person
The evening of Fri Aug 28 — out into the dunes by camel for sunset, the image everyone pictures when they think of the Sahara. Not included in the Riad Madu booking or the driver's rate, so it needs asking for. Merzouga properties almost all run their own trek, and going through the riad is simpler and usually cheaper than an agency. Ask at the same time whether they offer an optional overnight in a desert camp — if we want the night under canvas, that's how to get it.

Nights Out — Dinner & a Show

The evenings that made the trip — one pick per remaining night

How these were chosen — read this first. I can't browse TikTok or see what's actually trending there, so I can't promise these are the viral spots. What I did instead: searched recent (2026) coverage and review counts, and kept the places that keep resurfacing across guides, Tripadvisor volume and social write-ups. Ishq and Fez & Friends genuinely came up through TikTok-related results; the rest are strong on recent reviews. Treat this as a well-researched shortlist, not a scraped ranking — and check current hours, since a few of these change their music nights.
Book them now, not on the day. Every one of these needs a reservation in peak season, and the two best (Ishq and Rick's) fill first. Our driver can drop and collect us at each — worth telling him the plan for the week.

Tonight · Wed Aug 26 · Marrakech

Safran by Kôya Already our plan ✓Show 9:00–11:45 pm
Exactly the format you two like — Moroccan and Mediterranean plates with a full evening show: oriental dancers, Gnawa musicians and a DJ set that runs to nearly midnight. 📍 Rue Jbel Lakhdar, in the medina · lamb tagine with prunes, saffron seafood risotto, grilled octopus ⚠️ Reservations are essential — confirm today if it isn't already locked.
safranmarrakech.com Book a table

Thu Aug 27 & Fri Aug 28 · The desert

No restaurant hunting on these two nights. Boumalne Dades has very little open after dark, so eat at Eden Boutique — tell them at check-in. The Merzouga night is the one to make special: the sunset camel trek followed by dinner and Berber drumming under the stars is the whole point, and Riad Madu can arrange it. That's still unbooked.

Sat Aug 29 · Fes, arrival night

Eat in at Riad Fes After a 7-hour driveRelais & Châteaux
We arrive from Merzouga in the evening, tired. Riad Fes is a Relais & Châteaux with its own rooftop and one of the loveliest courtyards in the medina — a soft landing beats hunting for a table. If we've got energy: Mouda Palace runs live entertainment on Saturday nights, and Aug 29 is a Saturday. Only worth it if the drive doesn't flatten us.

Mon Aug 31 · Fes

Palais Bab Sahra Most beautiful roomPalace dining
A restored 19th-century palace in the heart of the medina — one of the most visually stunning rooms in Fes, and the kind of place that photographs like a film set. Classic Fassi cooking: lamb bastilla, slow-cooked mechoui. Alternative: Fez & Friends has live music through peak dinner hours and a lighter, livelier feel — the other name that came up repeatedly on TikTok.

Thu Sep 3 · Casablanca — the last night

Rick's Café ★ The right ending~$44/person
This is the La Pergola jazz night, Moroccan edition — live jazz most evenings, vintage pianos, mosaic tile and candlelit tables, built as the bar from the 1942 film. Recent August 2026 reviews single out the shows. 📍 Boulevard Sour Jdid, by the old medina — about 10–15 minutes from Barceló Anfa, and our driver has us. ⚠️ Book ahead, and go early. Our flight is at 4:00 am and we leave the hotel around 1:30 am, so this is an early dinner and a real set — not a late one.
📍 Google Maps
If we'd rather have the ocean: Le Cabestan has sat on the Corniche since 1927 — modern Mediterranean, floor-to-ceiling Atlantic views, and much closer to Barceló Anfa. Beautiful, but no show. Rick's is the better last night for us.

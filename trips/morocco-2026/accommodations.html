⏰ The 4:00 am flight is the thing to plan here. Barceló Anfa is a city hotel, not an airport hotel — there's no 24/7 shuttle to fall back on and it's a 30-minute drive to CMN. We need to be leaving the hotel by about 1:30 am, which is well before the 07:00 checkout desk opens. Three things to sort with the hotel in advance, ideally when we check in: (1) book the airport car through them for 1:30 am — don't plan on hailing one at 1 am; (2) ask to settle the bill the evening of Sep 3 so we're not waiting on a night clerk; (3) confirm someone is on the front desk overnight to let us out and hand back anything in the safe.

For reference only, now that we're booked: the Nouaceur airport cluster — Atlas Sky Airport, Relax Hotel Airport and ONOMO Casablanca Airport — all sit minutes from the terminal with free 24/7 shuttles. Worth knowing if anything changes, but the Barceló rate is no longer refundable.

Our Driver

The Big Picture

No trains, no buses, no transfers to book — one driver has all of it

This page used to compare trains, buses and one-off transfers for four separate legs. It doesn't need to any more: we've hired one private driver for the entire trip, from the moment we land at Casablanca on Aug 24 to the 1:30 am run back to the airport on Sep 4. Same car, same driver, every day.

What this actually buys us: door-to-door on every leg with luggage, no bus timetables in peak season, no hunting a taxi at 1 am for a 4 am flight, and a local who knows the roads for the two long mountain days. It also turns the five "city" days into optional day trips — the car is simply there.
⚠️ Confirm the day count and the inclusions. We're counting 11 days (Aug 24 through Sep 3) at MAD 1,700 = MAD 18,700. If he counts the pre-dawn Sep 4 airport run as a twelfth day, it's MAD 20,400. Settle that now rather than at the airport. Same for what the rate covers — fuel, tolls, and above all his own room and meals for eleven nights, which is a far bigger question than it was for three.

What It Costs

MAD 1,700 a day, held at the old rate despite the fuel-price rise

Private car & driver — whole trip ✓ ArrangedMAD 18,700
MAD 1,700/day × 11 days (Aug 24 – Sep 3) = MAD 18,700 ≈ $2,011 at the 9.30 MAD/$ rate he quoted That works out to about $183 a day, or $91 each, for a private car and driver on call. Paid in dirhams, in cash — plan a proper withdrawal early rather than a string of ATM trips. ⚠️ If the Sep 4 airport run counts as a twelfth day, it's MAD 20,400 (~$2,194). Agree which it is before we go.
How it compares. Booking this piecemeal — airport transfer (~$135), the 3-day desert driver (~$548), Fes→Chefchaouen (~$115), Chefchaouen→Casablanca (~$165) and the 1:30 am airport car (~$30) — came to roughly $993. So the full-trip arrangement costs about $1,000 more, and what that extra buys is the car sitting with us on five city days.
So use those five days. Aug 25–26, Aug 30–31 and Sep 2 are days when the medinas are car-free and he'd otherwise be waiting — that's around MAD 8,500 (~$914) of the total. Turn them into the day trips above (Ourika Valley, Meknès & Volubilis, Akchour waterfalls) and the arrangement pays for itself; leave the car parked and we're paying a premium for convenience on the travel days. Worth asking whether he'd take a reduced standby rate for city days if we'd rather keep them free.

If Anything Falls Through

Reference only — the public options we no longer need to book

Keeping this here in case the driver cancels close to the date. Morocco's public transport is genuinely good, and every leg of our route is doable without a car:

◆ CMN → Marrakech

An ONCF train runs from inside the airport, changing at Casa-Voyageurs. ~$12–14 each, 3–3.5 hrs.

◆ Marrakech → Fes

Direct ONCF train, ~7 hrs, ~$20 each — but it skips the desert entirely, so this is a last resort.

◆ Fes → Chefchaouen

No train exists. CTM or Supratours coaches, ~$8–10 each, ~4–4.5 hrs.

◆ Chefchaouen → Casablanca

CTM to Tangier then the Al Boraq high-speed train, ~$25–35 each, ~5–6 hrs. Or a direct coach, ~$12.

Private transfer companies like Welcome Pickups and Daytrip can cover single legs at short notice, and the vetted Sahara operators could pick up the desert days.
